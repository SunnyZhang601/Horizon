### Frontier Chips for Frontier AI Labs, with Walter Goodwin, Founder/CEO of Fractile

https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

The Takeaway: Walter Goodwin, founder and CEO of the full-stack AI chip company Fractile, told the No Priors podcast that the industry's fixation on making chatbots feel snappier misses where the real demand is going. "The snappier chatbot is kind of the faster horses of fast inference," he said, arguing that long-running agents, not human impatience, are what will strain today's hardware. Fractile, which he started in the summer of 2022 and now counts roughly 150 people, made its first bet on SRAM-based designs in the vein of Groq and Cerebras, then pivoted toward giving chips very high bandwidth access to DRAM. Goodwin claims around 25x more bandwidth per chip than an HBM-based design, which he frames as enabling "scaling laws for bandwidth": enough memory bandwidth to make sparse, enormous MoE models cheaply servable. He sees the chip landscape as an "extraordinary zoo" of identikit parts, with Google's TPU, Meta's MTIA, Microsoft's Maia and OpenAI's in-house accelerator all flowing through the same ASIC houses, built from the same HBM and tensor cores and the same TSMC packaging. His counter is AI-forward chip design: a top-three semiconductor CEO told him it takes ten years to go from architect intent to GDS2, and Goodwin says "divide it by four on time scale." "The equivalent of the frontier model for the chip space is if you can just find a way to structurally carve out a three to six months advantage, you will be winning all of those deployments," he said. He also argued that frontier labs need supply diversity, because going all-in on one vendor's proprietary silicon is an asymmetric risk, and noted that NVIDIA systems already ship with six to nine custom chips inside. Fractile's platform ramps in the second half of next year.

核心要点：全栈 AI 芯片公司 Fractile 的创始人兼 CEO Walter Goodwin 在 No Priors 播客上说，业界整天盯着"把聊天机器人变得更快"，其实搞错了真正需求的走向。他说 "The snappier chatbot is kind of the faster horses of fast inference"（更快的聊天机器人，就像汽车时代来临前的快马），真正会压垮现有硬件的是长时间运行的 agent，而不是人类的急性子。Fractile 是他在 2022 年夏天创办的，目前约 150 人；第一版押注的是类似 Groq 和 Cerebras 的 SRAM 路线，后来转向让芯片以极高带宽访问 DRAM。他声称每颗芯片的带宽比 HBM 方案高约 25 倍，并把这称为解锁了 "scaling laws for bandwidth"：内存带宽足够大，稀疏的超大 MoE 模型才能被低成本地伺服。他把如今的芯片市场形容为一个 "extraordinary zoo"（千奇百怪的动物园）：Google 的 TPU、Meta 的 MTIA、Microsoft 的 Maia 和 OpenAI 的自研加速器，全都出自同一批 ASIC 设计公司，用同样的 HBM、同样的 tensor core、同样的 TSMC 封装。他的不同答案是 AI-forward 的芯片设计：一位排名前三的半导体 CEO 告诉他，从架构意图到 GDS2 要十年，Goodwin 的回应是"把时间除以四"。他还放了一句很硬的话："The equivalent of the frontier model for the chip space is if you can just find a way to structurally carve out a three to six months advantage, you will be winning all of those deployments"（芯片界的 frontier model，就是看你能不能结构性地领先三到六个月，从而赢下所有部署）。他同时提醒前沿实验室需要供应链的多样性，因为全押某一家厂商的专有芯片是不对称的风险；而 NVIDIA 的系统里其实已经装了六到九颗定制芯片。Fractile 的平台将在明年下半年开始放量。
