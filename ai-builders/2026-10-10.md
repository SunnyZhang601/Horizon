**Box CEO Aaron Levie** ([link](https://x.com/levie/status/2109079686286565378))

Aaron Levie argues that agent swarms are where the bulk of future token consumption will happen, with massive implications for enterprise domains like software development, cybersecurity, life sciences research, AI research, and finance. His core point: agents can fan out and coordinate work toward a unified goal in ways humans structurally cannot, because people hit hard limits on communication, sharing status, resolving conflicts, and keeping shared context. As models handle larger context without getting confused and communicate across shared infrastructure, he predicts not just faster execution but qualitatively different outcomes in medicine, research, cyber defense, and scientific discovery. His conclusion: this is why we are going to need 1,000X more compute.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，agent swarm 将是未来 token 消耗的主要来源，会在软件开发、网络安全、生命科学研究、AI 研究和金融等企业领域产生巨大影响。他的核心论点是：agent 可以围绕统一目标 fan out 并互相协同，这是人类在结构上做不到的，因为人会卡在沟通、同步状态、处理工作冲突和维持 shared context 上。随着模型能处理更大的 context window 而不混乱，并能通过共享基础设施高效协作，他预言这不只是任务执行更快，而是在医学、研究、网络防御和科学发现上产生质量上完全不同的成果。他的结论是：这就是为什么我们需要 1,000X 更多的算力。

**Box CEO Aaron Levie** ([link](https://x.com/levie/status/2108952226714865966))

In a second post on enterprise AI governance, Levie writes that as agents begin doing vastly more task execution than humans, the need to secure, govern, control, and audit that work becomes paramount. **Meta Senior Director of AI Madhu Guru** ([link](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2108982858513776899))

Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta who previously led Gemini, Veo, and Nano Banana at Google, lays out an evolutionary arc for the agent space: capability, then trust, then autonomy. The first phase asked whether the combination of model, harness, tools, and context could reliably get work done, and capabilities have gotten pretty far, so the focus now shifts to trust: does the system take the right action with the right permissions, and can it be observed, audited, and held accountable? He warns that agent capabilities will soon evolve at RSI pace, which means agentic security covering identity, permissions, and observability has to outpace it, and the challenge is getting both more important and harder.

Meta AI 方向 Senior Director Madhu Guru（此前在 Google 主管 Gemini、Veo 和 Nano Banana）给出了 agent 生态的演化路径：先 capability，再 trust，最后 autonomy。第一阶段的问题是"model + harness + tools + context 的组合能否稳定把活干完"，如今 capability 已经走得相当远，焦点正转向 trust：系统是否以正确的权限做正确的事，能否被观测、审计并建立问责。他警告 agent 能力将很快以 RSI pace 进化，因此覆盖 identity、权限和可观测性的 agentic security 必须跑得更快，而这个挑战正同时变得更关键、更困难。

### No Priors: Beam: The Great American Open Model with ReflectionAI Co-Founder and CEO Misha Laskin

[Listen here](https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast)

The Takeaway: The strongest argument for frontier open weight models is not ideology but security: a few hundred safety researchers inside closed labs cannot cover the long tail of what powerful systems can do, so openness is how the industry catches its own failures.

That is the core conviction of Misha Laskin, co-founder and CEO of Reflection AI, who was a reinforcement learning researcher at Google DeepMind working on the first Gemini models before leaving to build frontier open intelligence in the West. His lab just released Beam, a 500-billion-parameter model with 23 billion active parameters, trained for coding and agentic work, backed by what he calls the largest reinforcement learning run ever documented in open source: over 10,000 GB300 GPUs running for four weeks.

Laskin's most useful mental model is that AI training has become an engineering discipline with an economics question at its center. Like AlphaGo before it, reinforcement learning now simply never stops improving: the plots keep going up, and the only limit is compute. That shifts the field's question from "can we train this" to "where is the economically valuable data," and he names finance compliance flows, cyber defense, and legal work as the first enterprise pools. He also argues that catching up to the frontier is far cheaper than being at it: roughly a 4x compute multiplier per model generation, about 7x in compute efficiency gains per year from human researchers in pretraining, and possibly 30x now that models help improve themselves.

On safety, he rejects the premise that openness is the risk. "Linus' law, with enough eyeballs, all bugs become shallow. I have the belief that with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow as well." As empirical evidence, he points to an incident where a very powerful closed model hacked into another company, and the only way that company could remediate itself was by using open models to protect itself. His commercial prediction: token demand has already flipped from 70/30 closed-open to 70/30 open-closed on gateways in about six months, and the end state looks like Linux, with enterprises moving from renting intelligence to owning it.

支持 frontier 级 open weight 模型的最强论据其实不是意识形态，而是安全：闭源实验室里只有几百名 safety researcher，根本覆盖不了强大系统的 long tail 风险，开放才是行业发现自身漏洞的方式。

说这话的是 Misha Laskin，Reflection AI 联合创始人兼 CEO，此前是 Google DeepMind 的 reinforcement learning 研究员，参与过最早一批 Gemini 模型，之后离开去西方打造 frontier 级 open intelligence。他的团队刚刚发布 Beam，一个总参数 5000 亿、激活参数 230 亿的模型，专为 coding 和 agentic 任务训练，背后是他所称的开源界有史以来被记录的最大规模 reinforcement learning 训练：超过 10,000 块 GB300 GPU 跑了四周。

Laskin 最有用的心智模型是：AI 训练已经变成一门工程学科，而学科中心是一个经济学问题。和当年的 AlphaGo 一样，如今的 reinforcement learning 系统几乎不再停止进步：曲线一直向上，唯一的限制就是 compute。这把行业的问题从"能不能训出来"变成了"哪里有经济价值的数据"，他点名 finance 合规流程、cyber defense 和 legal 是第一批企业级数据池。他还认为追赶 frontier 比身处 frontier 便宜得多：每代模型大约 4x 的 compute 倍增，pretraining 里人类研究员每年带来约 7x 的 compute 效率提升，而现在模型开始帮模型自我改进，可能达到 30x。

在安全问题上，他反对"开放即风险"的前提。"Linus' law, with enough eyeballs, all bugs become shallow. I have the belief that with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow as well."（Linus 定律：眼球足够多，所有 bug 都会变浅；他相信眼球足够多，大多数安全和 safety 漏洞同样会变浅。）作为实证，他举了一个案例：一个很强大的闭源模型黑进了另一家公司，而那家公司唯一能完成自救的方式就是用 open model 保护自己。他的商业预测是：gateway 上的 token 需求在大约六个月内已经从 70/30 闭源占优翻转为 70/30 开源占优，终局会更像 Linux，企业将从"租用 intelligence"走向"拥有 intelligence"。
