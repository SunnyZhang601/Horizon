**Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta, previously led Gemini, Veo, and Nano Banana at Google**

Madhu Guru published part 10 of his evals series: measure the steps, not just the result. Using a high school math analogy, he compared two agent trajectories that reach the same answer, one with 4 clean tool calls and one with 17 calls and retries, and showed why the first is the better system. His recipe: define the whole workflow, define the tasks per step, decide how to measure each step, and define median and hard tasks. Study the steps first, results next.

Madhu Guru（Meta AI 高级总监，曾在 Google 负责 Gemini、Veo 和 Nano Banana）发布了他的 evals 系列第 10 篇：衡量过程，而不只看结果。他借高中数学类比，对比了两条抵达同一答案的 agent 轨迹，一条只用了 4 次干净利落的 tool call，另一条用了 17 次调用和重试，并说明为什么前者才是更好的系统。他的方法：先定义整个工作流，再定义每一步的任务，决定每一步怎么测，最后定义中等和困难的任务。先研究步骤，再研究结果。

**When AI Improves Itself | Richard Socher (Recursive)** (The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck)

The Takeaway: AI is about to rebuild how science itself gets done, and the mechanism is precise: any domain that can be simulated and verified is a domain AI will eventually master, and that is what makes recursive self improvement real rather than hype.

Richard Socher, one of the most cited researchers in AI and now CEO of Recursive, just raised 650 million dollars and published a book, The Eureka Machine. His core argument starts with a puzzle: scientific progress has slowed. Knowledge has become "a labyrinth", with 34,000 journals that "might as well have no trespassing signs", and no single human can be the Renaissance generalist anymore. AI, he says, is what calculus was to physics, the tool that weaves the pieces back together. The engine of his optimism is a rule: "Anything you can simulate, AI will solve." Games fell first, then programming, where you can verify output, and math is next. For biology, the frontier is data: perturbation studies at scale, organoids, and eventually a virtual cell where AI can experiment millions of times. He addresses the scary word directly: hallucination is a feature when you want novel proteins and a bug when you want facts, and the fact problem was largely fixed by grounding models in search. He rejects a hard takeoff, since real-world trials take real time, but notes bio startups now reach late-stage trials with multiple drugs in 6 to 18 months instead of one drug in 8 years. On jobs, his test is elasticity of demand: illustration prices fell 1000x and demand did not, so illustrators suffered, while coding got cheaper and demand exploded through the Jevons paradox. The Eureka Machine rests on four pillars: LLM ingesting human knowledge, scientific measurements beyond human perception, simulation, and robotic labs, with an agent swarm on top. His parting thought: we are nowhere near the upper bounds of intelligence, in any of its ten forms.

Richard Socher 是被引用最多的 AI 研究者之一，现任 Recursive CEO，刚刚融资 6.5 亿美元，并出版了新书《The Eureka Machine》。他的核心论证从一个谜题开始：科学进步在变慢。知识已经变成"一座迷宫"，34,000 份期刊"简直像挂满了禁止入内的牌子"，再也没有人能当文艺复兴式的通才。他说，AI 之于今天的科学，就像微积分之于物理学，是把碎片重新编织起来的工具。他乐观的引擎是一条规则："Anything you can simulate, AI will solve."（凡是能被模拟的东西，AI 终将解决。）游戏先倒下，然后是可以验证输出的编程，接下来是数学。对生物学来说，前沿在数据：大规模的 perturbation 研究、organoids，以及最终一个可供 AI 做百万次实验的 virtual cell。他直面那个吓人的词：想要生成全新蛋白质时，hallucination 是 feature，想要事实时它是 bug，而事实问题大部分已经靠接搜索引擎解决了。他不相信 hard takeoff，因为现实中的试验需要真实的时间，但他指出生物初创公司如今 6 到 18 个月就能带着多个药物进入后期试验，而不是 8 年只出一个药。关于就业，他的判断标准是需求弹性：插画价格跌了 1000 倍而需求没有涨，插画师因此受损；编程变便宜后需求通过 Jevons paradox 爆发。The Eureka Machine 有四大支柱：吞下人类知识的 LLM、超越人类感知的科学测量、simulation，以及机器人实验室，最上面再架一层 agent swarm。他的结语是：在十种智能形态的任何一种上，我们都离真正的上限还非常远。

**Coinbase's Everything Exchange: Agentic Finance, Stablecoins, and Tokenization with CEO Brian Armstrong** (No Priors)

The Takeaway: the next wave of crypto adoption is not human, it is agentic, and stablecoin rails exist precisely because 76 percent of agent transactions are too small for card networks to touch.

Brian Armstrong, cofounder and CEO of Coinbase, laid out the three big bets: the Everything Exchange, where stocks, commodities, crypto, derivatives, and prediction markets converge onchain with good liquidity and cross margin; stablecoin payments, moving money anywhere in under a second for under a cent; and Agentic Finance, or AIFi. The most striking number: the minimum card fee is about 30 cents, and "about 76% of the agent ecommerce transactions we're seeing are under 30¢". His answer is to give agents their own accounts: "We don't want the AIs to be unbanked." A single pasted prompt gives an agent a self-custodial wallet, no KYC possible since agents have no government ID. Payments flow over X402, the protocol Coinbase incubated and moved into the Linux Foundation, now backed by Google, Cloudflare, and AWS. Inside Coinbase, Armstrong described pushing toward recursive self improvement: every repository has a "brain", a markdown history of incidents, financial controls, and pull requests, and every human correction to an agent's output flows back into it so one-shot accept rates keep rising. He even ships features himself now: before recording, ten parallel agents, several of them Grok, finished phase one of a feature plan he requested. "It's very addictive." Beyond agents, he sees tokenization updating the whole financial system, from tokenized stocks for the 4 billion people without brokerage access to prediction markets on policy outcomes and even the big life questions. Crypto powered about half of one percent of global GDP, and he thinks the job is just getting started.

Brian Armstrong（Coinbase 联合创始人兼 CEO）给出了三大押注：Everything Exchange，让股票、大宗商品、crypto、衍生品和 prediction market 在链上汇聚，具备流动性和 cross margin；stablecoin 支付，一秒以内、不到一美分把钱送到全球任何地方；以及 Agentic Finance（AIFi）。最扎眼的数字是：银行卡的最低费用约 30 美分，而"about 76% of the agent ecommerce transactions we're seeing are under 30¢"（我们所见的 agent 电商交易里约 76% 不到 30 美分）。他的解法是给 agent 自己的账户："We don't want the AIs to be unbanked."（我们不想让 AI 成为无银行账户者。）粘贴一条 prompt 就能让 agent 拥有自托管钱包，因为 agent 没有政府证件，KYC 也无从谈起。支付走 X402 协议，这个协议由 Coinbase 孵化并捐给 Linux Foundation，如今 Google、Cloudflare 和 AWS 都在参与。在公司内部，Armstrong 描述了推进 recursive self improvement 的做法：每个代码仓库都有一个"brain"，一份记录事故、财务控制和 pull request 的 markdown 历史，人类对 agent 输出的每次修正都会回流进去，让 one-shot PR 的接受率持续上升。他本人现在甚至亲自发版：录节目前，他让十个并行 agent（其中不少是 Grok）把他要的 feature 第一阶段全做完了。"It's very addictive."（非常上瘾。）在 agent 之外，他看到 tokenization 正在更新整个金融系统：从为全球 40 亿没有券商账户的人提供 tokenized 股票，到对政策结果、甚至对人生大问题的 prediction market。crypto 目前只支撑全球 GDP 的约 0.5%，他认为一切才刚刚开始。
