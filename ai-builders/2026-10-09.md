AI 教程作者 Peter Yang 对当下的创业风向提出质疑：是不是该停止再造更多管理邮件的个人助理，也别再开更多号称能管理、治愈疾病的 AI 公司了。他点出了 AI 产品赛道高度同质化的问题。https://x.com/petergyang/status/2108679515681722787

**Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta, previously led Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana at Google**

Madhu Guru argues that open-weight models did not start the drop in price per unit of intelligence; they are a tailwind on a trend that was already underway. He traces the decline to three forces: model builders distilling their best models into smaller ones with cheaper inference, infrastructure efficiency gains, and competition between model providers. The pattern shows up at every model company, he notes: version x of the mid-size model matches version x-1 of the large model, same intelligence for less money. https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2108618266776387886

Meta AI 方向 Senior Director Madhu Guru（此前在 Google 主管 Gemini、Veo 和 Nano Banana）提出：open-weight 模型并不是"单位智能价格下降"的起点，它们只是给一个已经在发生的趋势加了一把火。他把降价归因于三个因素：一是模型厂商把最强模型蒸馏成更小、推理更便宜的模型；二是基础设施效率提升；三是模型厂商之间的竞争。他补充道，这个规律在每家模型公司都成立：中型模型 version x 的智能水平等于上一代大模型 version x-1，同样的智能，更低的价格。https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2108618266776387886

**Aaron Levie, CEO of Box**

Aaron Levie argues that agents spawning other agents, agents operating in the background on every task, and agent swarms will consume 1,000X more tokens than people prompting agents one at a time. Using AI as a chat system that can only work at the pace you can prompt it will seem like a relic within a year or two, he predicts, with the vast majority of tokens consumed by agents doing continuous work in the background and in workflows. That is why we are still so early in the curve of AI agent adoption, and why much more compute and infrastructure buildout will be needed. https://x.com/levie/status/2108750943680630893

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，agent 繁衍 agent、agent 在后台处理每一项任务、以及 agent swarm，消耗的 token 会比人们一个一个手动 prompt 多出 1,000X。他预测，把 AI 当作只能按你打字速度干活的聊天系统，一两年内就会显得像老古董；绝大多数 token 将被那些在后台和 workflow 中持续工作的 agent 消耗掉。这也是为什么我们仍处在 AI agent 采用曲线的极早期，未来还需要多得多的算力和基础设施扩建。https://x.com/levie/status/2108750943680630893
