**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck: "Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer | Renen Hallak, VAST Data"**

The Takeaway: Matt Turck interviews Renen Hallak, founder and CEO of VAST Data, the software infrastructure company valued at 30 billion dollars that quietly powers xAI and many of the big AI clouds. The framing is Jensen Huang's five layer AI cake: power, chips, software infrastructure, models and applications. The middle layer, especially software infrastructure, is the least understood, and that is where VAST lives, calling itself the operating system for this new era. Hallak explains the AI factory concept: racks went from 10kW to 500kW, and he believes enterprises will eventually all need AI factories, with their most valuable IP distilled into model weights. His favorite analogy is human: we infer during the day, we fine-tune at night, and agents are like artificial people that keep fine-tuning models from experience. This week VAST announced model management plus confidential computing through DataEnclave, using NVIDIA encrypted memory so enterprises can run inference on premises with neither data nor weights exposed. On architecture, VAST's DASE, disaggregated shared everything, rejects sharding, and one customer cluster spans multiple exabytes delivering tens of terabytes per second. Training is simple compared to inference, because if training goes down nobody notices, but if inference is down there is no service, which is why inference needs model routing, KV caches, RAG and agent memory with per-agent access control. Demand keeps surprising him, in his words, "Sometimes it scares me. We had a customer, one of these AI clouds, they said we're probably going to need about 500 petabytes over the next three years. Last week, came back to us and said, we're gonna need an extra two exabytes on top of that 500 petabytes." He expects AI clouds sold out for the next year and a half, sees sovereign AI becoming a basic need like electricity and water, and notes VAST is a profitable software business growing roughly 10x every two years, with NVIDIA as its best partner though, in his words, "other than as an investor there's no legal document between us and Nvidia".

Matt Turck 采访了 VAST Data 的创始人兼 CEO Renen Hallak，这家估值 300 亿美元的 software infrastructure 公司在幕后支撑着 xAI 和众多大型 AI 云。访谈的框架是 Jensen Huang 的五层 AI cake：power、chips、software infrastructure、models、applications。中间层尤其是 software infrastructure 最少被人理解，而 VAST 正活在这里，自称这个新纪元的 operating system。Hallak 解释了 AI factory 概念：机柜功率从 10kW 涨到 500kW，他相信企业最终都需要 AI factory，而企业最值钱的 IP 会蒸馏进 model weights。他最喜欢的类比来自人脑：白天我们做 inference，晚上我们做 fine-tune，而 agent 就像 "人造人"，不断用经验微调模型。本周 VAST 宣布了 model management 加上通过 DataEnclave 实现的 confidential computing，利用 NVIDIA 加密内存，让企业在本地跑 inference 时不暴露数据也不暴露权重。在架构上，VAST 的 DASE（disaggregated shared everything）拒绝了分片，单一客户集群跨多个 exabyte、吞吐达数十 TB/s。相比 inference，training 反而简单：training 挂了没人发现，inference 挂了就是没有服务，因此 inference 需要 model routing、KV cache、RAG 以及带 per-agent 访问控制的 agent memory。需求一直在让他吃惊，原话是 "Sometimes it scares me. **No Priors: "Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era with Sequence Holdings Co-Founder and CEO Michael Lee"**

The Takeaway: Michael Lee, cofounder and CEO of Sequence Holdings, explains a new kind of deal: the largest AI take private to date, announced with the Dell family office, buying Baldwin, an insurance brokerage, for 7.7 billion dollars. Sequence is a permanent holding company that partners with management teams to buy and "refound" incumbent businesses as AI era market leaders, one deal a year with no deployment cadence. His background runs from Goldman to Apollo to Lone Pine, where he covered AI from 2017, through AlphaGo and the first transformer paper, and ChatGPT at the end of 2022 showed him an architecture that could scale infinitely. His thesis: AI's impact will be uneven, coding goes to startups, but some industries have incumbents with all the advantages, so if you could acquire the right incumbent and inherit those advantages, could you forge the market leader? The holding company structure exists to align capital duration with sustained investment, and because in a world where alpha comes from engineering and AI, the celebrated persona must be the engineer, not the investor. He walks through the BankSouth pilot with family friend Jamie Reynolds, cofounder of Avenir: the regulated nature of the bank was a feature, not a bug, with clean data and well-defined rules ideal for agents, and dense centralized operations amortize builds. Results: consumer underwriting time down 94% since March, commercial loans from 30 days to 11 days, and Q2 loan volumes doubled while the bank absorbed it with a smaller underwriting team. The Atlas platform stacks a data ontology, an agent builder, Lattice orchestration and an application builder. On why Baldwin: insurance is a two trillion dollar market where carriers make money investing premiums, brokers keep 90% gross retention with no price competition, and the business is, in his words, "completely immune to what the model's going to do. As the model performances improve, we celebrate." His advice closes the episode: ideas are cheap and execution is really hard, so bet on exceptional people in big markets.

Michael Lee 是 Sequence Holdings 的联合创始人兼 CEO，他讲述了一笔新型交易：与 Dell 家族办公室共同宣布的、迄今最大的 AI take private，以 77 亿美元收购保险经纪公司 Baldwin。Sequence 是一家永续型 holding company，与管理层合作买入并 "重新创立"（refound）传统行业公司，目标是 AI 时代的市场领导者，一年只做一单，没有部署节奏压力。他的履历从 Goldman 到 Apollo 再到 Lone Pine，2017 年起就覆盖 AI，经历 AlphaGo 和第一篇 transformer 论文，而 2022 年底的 ChatGPT 让他看到了一个可以无限 scale 的架构。他的论点：AI 的影响将是不均匀的，编码会流向创业公司，但在某些行业 incumbent 拥有全部优势，所以如果你能收购合适的 incumbent 并继承这些优势，能否锻造出市场领导者？选择 holding company 结构是为了让资本的期限匹配持续投入，也因为在一个 alpha 来自工程与 AI 的世界里，被庆祝的偶像应该是工程师而不是投资人。他详细讲了与家族好友、Avenir 联合创始人 Jamie Reynolds 一起做的 BankSouth 试点：银行受监管的属性是 feature 不是 bug，干净的数据和清晰的规则对 agent 极为友好，而密集集中化的运营能摊薄建设成本。成果：3 月以来 consumer underwriting 时间缩短 94%，commercial loan 从 30 天降到 11 天，Q2 贷款量翻倍时银行用更小的 underwriting 团队就接住了。Atlas 平台的层次是：data ontology、agent builder、Lattice 编排、application builder。选择 Baldwin 的理由：保险是超过 2 万亿美元的市场，carriers 靠投资保费赚钱、不靠承保，broker 的 gross retention 高达 90% 且没有价格战，用他的话说这个生意 "对模型的表现完全免疫，模型越强我们越高兴"。他给创业者的收尾建议：ideas are cheap and execution is really hard，所以要在大市场里押注顶尖的人。
