**Peter Yang, practical AI tutorials and interviews for busy people**

In a long "armchair" thread on the personal agent race, he argues Muse is poised to become the leader: the app is intuitive, Meta is promoting it aggressively, and an agent simply does not make sense inside an existing messaging app. He thinks ChatGPT is still the personal agent leader with 1B+ users and arguably better models, computer use and voice, but the work/personal split is confusing enough that he uses Grok Bot for work. Grok Bot is not a Muse competitor since it is work-focused; his bull case is that it is a few features away from being multiplayer agentic Slack. Google is the dark horse: personal agents live off Gmail, Calendar and Google data, but Spark is still a secondary tab in Gemini and Google's multiplayer AI track record is weak. On multiplayer itself, nobody has solved it, and he thinks it is the next big unlock. His current stack: Muse for personal, Grok Bot for cloud tasks, Claude for specific use cases, ChatGPT for everything else. His closing advice: design your personal skills and files so they port easily between harnesses and agents, or you will spend half your time migrating between tools.

**Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel**

He describes the thoroughness with which agents test and QA software as unrivaled: when he pointed out something was not rendering right in a mobile in-app browser, the agent went to the depths of Mordor to reproduce, simulate, fix, deploy and verify, even creating an ephemeral Vercel deployment to throw at an iPhone simulator. Humans could never match this intensity; we run out of energy, extrapolate, and hope it works out. His conclusion: the software of the future will be of a quality and performance we have never experienced.

他形容 agent 测试和 QA 软件的彻底程度无与伦比：当他指出某个内容在移动端 in-app browser 里渲染异常时，agent 一路深入 "魔多" 去复现、模拟、修复、部署、验证，甚至创建了一个临时的 Vercel deployment 扔给 iPhone simulator。人类根本无法匹敌这种强度：我们会没精力、会脑补、会祈祷它能跑。他的结论：未来软件的质量和性能将是我们从未体验过的。
