### 🤖 机器人
**① PHRetune：把「阻抗增益怎么调」从试错变成离线计算**
- 话题：扩散策略 / VLA 输出的是动作表示，真正落到力矩由下游阻抗控制器完成，而刚度与阻尼增益往往**直接沿用数据采集时的设置**；传统做法靠反复 rollout 做经验扫描，成本高。
- 分析：该工作用端口-哈密顿（能量/力视角）从演示里估出「力与能量预算」，再把策略套到记录的观测上做比对，从而**闭式解出一个增益缩放系数**——增益在评估前就固定，不需要奖励、不需要重训、不需要运行时额外算力。
- 来源：[arXiv:2610.05755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05755)

**② MagCilia：把「多轴力 + 表面识别」塞进一个紧凑单元**
- 话题：磁纤毛受力形变 → 磁场变化 → 霍尔传感器非接触读取，单个紧凑结构同时拿到三维力与接触表面信息。
- 分析：值得注意的不是准确率数字（RMSE 0.4 ~ 0.7 N 量级），而是**架构选择**：用磁+霍尔做非接触式读取，只用「当前磁场 + 近期变化量」两个输入做回归，工程上比多传感单元阵列更容易小型化与批量标定。
- 来源：[arXiv:2610.09536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09536)

**③ 本窗口机器人另有两条 8.0 值得记名（仅列，不展开）**：PanTact（可穿戴接触接口 → 全身接触操作，**接触作为跨本体可迁移信息**）· AlphaNIST（**只用稀疏二元奖励**在 20 项装配任务上从零学到单一策略，仅仿真）。两者都指向同一个方向：**减少对「精心设计的中间表示」的依赖**——一个减掉机器人本体与身体传感器，一个减掉演示与进度奖励。

## 2. Deep Dives (Robotics · Embedded · EE/IC · AI)

### 🤖 Robotics
**① PHRetune: turning "how to tune impedance gains" from trial and error into offline computation**
- Topic: diffusion policies / VLAs output action representations; the actual mapping to torques is done by a downstream impedance controller, and the stiffness and damping gains are often **directly inherited from the settings used during data collection**; the traditional approach relies on repeated rollouts for empirical sweeps, which is costly.
- Analysis: the work estimates the "force and energy budget" from demonstrations using a port-Hamiltonian (energy/force) view, then replays the policy over recorded observations for comparison, thereby **solving in closed form for one gain scaling coefficient** — the gain is fixed before evaluation, with no rewards, no retraining, and no extra runtime compute.
- Source: [arXiv:2610.05755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05755)

**② MagCilia: packing "multi-axis force + surface recognition" into one compact unit**
- Topic: magnetic cilia deform under force → the magnetic field changes → Hall sensors read it contactlessly; a single compact structure captures 3D force and contact surface information at once.
- Analysis: what is notable is not the accuracy figures (RMSE on the order of 0.4 ~ 0.7 N) but the **architecture choice**: magnet + Hall for contactless readout, with only "current magnetic field + recent change" as the two regression inputs — easier to miniaturize and batch-calibrate in engineering than multi-sensor-unit arrays.
- Source: [arXiv:2610.09536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09536)

**① Terence Tao's "Math 2.0" and the "AI manuscript wave": the issue has moved from technical discussion to academic-ecosystem controversy**

Also a single preprint, not reproduced.
- Source: [arXiv:2610.12818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12818)
