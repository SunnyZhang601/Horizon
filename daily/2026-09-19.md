### 🤖 机器人

**1. 力控硬件的共同答案：低减速比 + 把质量赶到基座** — [DDD 9-DoF 直驱夹爪](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09198) 与 [AthenaZero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19194) 在同一窗口出现，解决的是同一个物理矛盾：高减速比换来扭矩，却同时带来摩擦、反射惯性和高机械阻抗，于是高速/高加速度的动态交互做不了。

- **分析**：两条路线都把「减速比」当成可调量而不是天花板 —— DDD 用 1:2 差速传动 + 两电机并联，在屈曲姿态下放大扭矩，把执行器质量集中到基座以降低运动连杆转动惯量；AthenaZero 更进一步，用传动远程化（transmission remotization）把电机从关节挪走。二者都强调**力矩透明性**，DDD 还给出「电机对系统惯量贡献 0.236%」和「断电被动阻抗 50.1 N/m」这类可量化指标，并明确把结论落在「为无传感器本体感知力估计提供基础」上。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2608.09198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09198) · [arXiv 2609.19194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19194)

**2. 只拿到标量测量时，姿态还收敛吗？** — [SO(3) 上的互补滤波用于标量测量姿态估计](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08099)

- **分析**：传统互补滤波假设每个惯性向量能给出完整的三维投影，而这篇文章处理的是**传感器只给出标量投影**（即某个已知惯性向量在机体坐标系某个传感方向上的投影）的情形，直接在 SO(3) 上设计观测器以避免欧拉角奇异性。结论是可执行的判据：当**至少三个惯性向量沿共同的机体坐标系方向**被测量且满足持续激励时，几乎全局渐近稳定；**只有两个标量测量**时，给出渐近收敛的充分条件与吸引域的显式刻画。局限是仅数值仿真，无硬件实验。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2604.08099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08099)

**3. 没有额外力/触觉硬件，怎么拿到「该用多大力」的标签** — [四通道双边遥操作实现无额外硬件的柔顺性辨识](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19976)

- **分析**：文章把问题归到可辨识性——实际位姿与测得力**无法区分**操作者的目标平衡位置与刚度，所以 VR 控制器、SpaceMouse、手持夹爪在原理上就给不出柔顺性监督。四通道双边遥操作把主臂当作「目标平衡位置」的独立测量，歧义被消掉，于是仅靠机械臂**已有的关节力矩传感**就能回归辨识出逐轴刚度，实现零标注成本、逐时间步、与方向相关的柔顺性标签，用于微调 VLA 使其同时输出位姿与刚度。Franka Research 3 擦拭任务上，五种策略里只有该方法在「用力擦」指令下改变了接触力（RMS 6.4N → 9.1N，Cohen's d=0.89，p=0.023）。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.19976](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19976)

**4. 形态与控制器联合设计：决定负载能力的是执行器包络，不是运动做没做出来** — [GLAMDRING：强化学习联合设计四足机器人与 CPG 步态](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19452)

- **分析**：给定前进速度边界、每执行器功率预算、执行器库与有效载荷要求，框架同时输出连杆几何 + 各关节执行器选型 + 基于 Hopf 振荡器的 CPG 步态策略，按最大速度 / 最小运输成本 / 最大载荷裕度排序候选。工程上最有价值的一条是：**决定可实现有效载荷能力的是「执行器包络可行性」，而非单纯的「运动成功」**；另有「多数设计中典型动物步态会自然涌现」。局限：预印本、未经同行评审、摘要未给具体数据。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.19452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19452)

**5. 仿真资产本身成了攻击面** — [碰撞网格投毒（CMP）](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18122)

- **分析**：3D 资产里视觉网格负责渲染、碰撞网格负责物理，两者**允许不一致**（为省算力可粗糙近似），这个「视觉—碰撞鸿沟」是合法且普遍存在的。攻击者只改碰撞网格，视觉与其他组件不动，被投毒资产可经合法供应链分发；策略在仿真里正常，真机上退化、失效或产生物理安全风险。作者评估的现有防御均不足以抵御。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.18122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18122)

- **分析**：昆虫的异步肌靠内在牵张激活自发振翅，无需神经信号；论文首次把异步（自激）驱动用到可飞行扑翼机器人上，机翼因此**无需控制输入**就能响应机体共振力学变化，并在**无外部传感**的情况下对障碍物碰撞做出瞬时反应，杂乱环境飞行测试中稳定性与性能均优于同步驱动。作者的框架是「低级驱动动力学」与「高级感觉运动系统」分工的具身控制。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.19480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19480)

**3. 逐事件、不攒帧：事件相机的低延迟路线** — [REACT：全脉冲状态空间模型](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19204)

- **分析**：多数事件相机学习方法先把事件累积成帧或时间箱，积分延迟就在那里产生；REACT 直接逐事件处理原始数据，核心是复数脉冲神经元 C-SiLIF，由物理事件间隔驱动连续时间动力学，状态以**单个事件的时间分辨率**演化。EvTTC 上相对 TTC 误差 9.59%、端到端延迟 4.6 ms（按平均接近速度折算约 4 cm 车辆位移，最快的竞争学习方法为 1 m），支持随时预测与零样本迁移，INT8 量化后每 32,768 事件能耗从 18.5 mJ 降到 2.8 mJ。无同行评审与独立验证。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.19204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19204)

**2. 信息的价值可以定义在「是否引导出同一个行动」上** — [实用信息的数学理论：扩展香农框架](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10986)

- **分析**：香农框架衡量的是符号保真度，不衡量信息对**后续决策**的价值；这篇用「等目标映射」把通向同一最优动作的不同语义路径视为实用等价，建立句法→语义→实用三层层级（每层抽象都丢掉与任务无关的区分），定义实用熵、上行/下行实用互信息，并证明无损信源编码、信道编码与率失真定理在终端动作层面的对应版本；再用 VoI（实用信息价值）与 CoI（实用信息成本）作为率失真的决策论对偶，给出跨层优化的拉格朗日对偶框架，实用效率界 `E_p(λ)=sup_R[Φ_p(R)−λCoI_p(R)]` 刻画给定资源价格下的最大净效用。预印本，无同行评审与应用证据。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.10986](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10986)

**3. 长时程 agent 的恢复：要回滚状态，还要留住经验** — [RIR：Rollback-Induced Reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304)

- **分析**：长时程 agent 的单个错误动作会改变后续状态与观测，错误随时间累积。已有恢复方法二分：修正上下文但不修复被改变的环境状态；回滚到早期状态却丢掉已获得的经验。RIR 把恢复当成「回滚边界控制」，联合决定**何时干预、从何处恢复、恢复后保留哪些信息**，从被放弃的轨迹里提炼可复用知识，并用回滚深度与保留记忆上的统一算子刻画恢复过程；三个长时程基准上报告在多 LLM 骨干上持续提升（未给具体基准与量化指标，效果待验证）。
- **来源**：[arXiv 2609.18304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304)

## 2. Deep Dives (Robotics · Embedded · EE/IC · AI)

### 🤖 Robotics

**1. The shared answer for force-controlled hardware: low reduction ratio + pushing mass to the base** — The [DDD 9-DoF direct-drive gripper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09198) and [AthenaZero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19194) appeared in the same window, solving the same physical contradiction: high reduction ratios buy torque but simultaneously bring friction, reflected inertia, and high mechanical impedance, so high-speed/high-acceleration dynamic interaction cannot be done.

- **Analysis**: both routes treat the "reduction ratio" as a tunable quantity rather than a ceiling — DDD uses 1:2 differential transmission + two motors in parallel, amplifying torque in flexed postures and concentrating actuator mass at the base to lower the moving links' rotational inertia; AthenaZero goes further, using transmission remotization to move the motors away from the joints. Both emphasize **torque transparency**, DDD also gives quantifiable metrics like "motors contribute 0.236% of system inertia" and "passive impedance when unpowered 50.1 N/m", and explicitly grounds the conclusion in "providing a basis for sensorless proprioceptive force estimation".
- **Sources**: [arXiv 2608.09198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09198) · [arXiv 2609.19194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19194)

**2. With only scalar measurements available, does attitude still converge?** — [Complementary filtering on SO(3) for attitude estimation from scalar measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08099)

- **Analysis**: traditional complementary filtering assumes each inertial vector gives a complete three-dimensional projection, whereas this paper handles the case where **sensors provide only a scalar projection** (i.e., the projection of a known inertial vector along some sensing direction in the body frame), designing the observer directly on SO(3) to avoid Euler-angle singularities. The conclusion is an executable criterion: when **at least three inertial vectors are measured along a common body-frame direction** satisfying persistent excitation, almost global asymptotic stability holds; with **only two scalar measurements**, sufficient conditions for asymptotic convergence and an explicit characterization of the region of attraction are given. The limitation: numerical simulation only, no hardware experiments.
- **Source**: [arXiv 2604.08099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08099)

**3. Without extra force/tactile hardware, how do you get labels for "how much force should be used"** — [Four-channel bilateral teleoperation achieves compliance identification with no extra hardware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19976)

- **Analysis**: the paper locates the problem in identifiability — with actual pose and measured force, the operator's target equilibrium position and stiffness **cannot be distinguished**, so VR controllers, SpaceMouse, and handheld grippers in principle cannot provide compliance supervision. Four-channel bilateral teleoperation treats the master arm as an independent measurement of the "target equilibrium position", eliminating the ambiguity, so compliance can be regressed and identified per-axis using only the robot arm's **existing joint torque sensing**, achieving zero-labeling-cost, per-timestep, direction-dependent compliance labels used to fine-tune a VLA so it outputs pose and stiffness simultaneously. On a Franka Research 3 wiping task, of five policies only this method changed contact force under the "wipe hard" instruction (RMS 6.4N → 9.1N, Cohen's d=0.89, p=0.023).
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.19976](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19976)

**4. Co-designing morphology and controller: what determines payload capacity is the actuator envelope, not whether locomotion was achieved** — [GLAMDRING: reinforcement learning co-designs quadruped robots and CPG gaits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19452)

- **Analysis**: given forward-speed bounds, per-actuator power budgets, an actuator library, and payload requirements, the framework simultaneously outputs link geometry + actuator selection per joint + a Hopf-oscillator-based CPG gait policy, ranking candidates by maximum speed / minimum cost of transport / maximum payload margin. The most engineering-valuable point: **what determines achievable payload capacity is "actuator envelope feasibility", not simply "locomotion succeeded"**; additionally, "in most designs, typical animal gaits emerge naturally". Limitations: preprint, not peer-reviewed, the abstract gives no concrete figures.
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.19452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19452)

**5. Simulation assets themselves became an attack surface** — [Collision-mesh poisoning (CMP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18122)

- **Analysis**: in a 3D asset the visual mesh handles rendering and the collision mesh handles physics, and the two **are allowed to disagree** (coarse approximation saves compute); this "visual–collision gap" is legitimate and pervasive. An attacker alters only the collision mesh, leaving visuals and other components untouched, and poisoned assets can be distributed through legitimate supply chains; the policy behaves normally in simulation but degrades, fails, or creates physical safety risks on real hardware. The existing defenses the authors evaluated all proved insufficient.
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.18122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18122)

### 🔌 Embedded

**1. MCUs with active anti-fault-injection still cannot withstand physical access** — [RP2350 secure debug broken by laser fault injection](https://donjon.ledger.com/blog/rp2350-secure-debug-laser-fault-injection/)

- **Analysis**: the RP2350 is among the first general-purpose MCUs to build active anti-fault-injection features (glitch detectors, redundant coprocessor) into a production chip, with secure boot + OTP serving as the foundation for key protection in DIY FIDO2 security key projects (Pico Keys and others). This attack was a combination of "photon-emission microscopy to locate hotspots + SWD-guided injection", bypassing secure debug protection and directly overturning those assumptions. What matters more is the cost judgment: the community believes the reproduction threshold is far below the original research's roughly USD 250,000 — a home lab can do it for under USD 10,000, and there is even a USD 50-class alternative.
- **Sources**: [Ledger Donjon blog](https://donjon.ledger.com/blog/rp2350-secure-debug-laser-fault-injection/) · [Community discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49757050)

**2. Pushing "reaction" down into the actuator layer: sensorless collision response in an asynchronously actuated flapping-wing robot** — [The first asynchronously actuated flapping-wing robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19480)

- **Analysis**: insects' asynchronous muscles self-oscillate the wings via stretch-activation without neural signals; the paper is the first to apply asynchronous (self-excited) actuation to a flying flapping-wing robot, so the wings respond to changes in the body's resonant mechanics **without control inputs**, and react instantaneously to obstacle collisions **without external sensing**; in cluttered-environment flight tests both stability and performance were better than synchronously actuated. The authors' framework is embodied control dividing labor between "low-level actuation dynamics" and "high-level sensorimotor systems".
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.19480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19480)

**3. Event-by-event, no frame accumulation: the low-latency route for event cameras** — [REACT: a fully spiking state space model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19204)

- **Analysis**: most event-camera learning methods first accumulate events into frames or time bins, and the integration latency arises right there; REACT processes raw data event-by-event directly, with the complex-valued spiking neuron C-SiLIF at its core, driven by physical event intervals with continuous-time dynamics, its state evolving at the **temporal resolution of a single event**. On EvTTC, relative TTC error is 9.59% and end-to-end latency 4.6 ms (about 4 cm of vehicle displacement at average approach speed, versus 1 m for the fastest competing learning method), supporting anytime prediction and zero-shot transfer, and after INT8 quantization energy per 32,768 events drops from 18.5 mJ to 2.8 mJ. No peer review or independent verification.
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.19204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19204)

### ⚡ EE/IC

**1. Depth-sensor rankings flip depending on the target being measured** — [Benchmarking commercial depth sensors for medical applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13028)

- **Analysis**: using stylus sampling as ground truth at about 50 cm distance, four modalities across four paths (Intel RealSense D405 stereo vision, PMD Flexx2 time-of-flight, Stereolabs ZED 2i stereo vision, Zivid 2M+ 60 structured light) were benchmarked on pig bone, pig abdomen specimens, and a silicone kidney phantom. Two conclusions: the Zivid 2M+ 60 was best on all targets and all metrics; **the ZED 2i ranked second on real tissue but last on the phantom** — rankings change with specimen type, because specimens contain three classes of real difficulties: uniform surfaces, specular reflection, and subsurface scattering.
- **Source**: [arXiv 2606.13028](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13028)

### 🧠 AI

**1. The acceptance problem for "AI proofs": the output must have an independent reviewer** — [Proving Conway's conjecture with AI vibe coding](https://overreacted.io/how-i-vibed-a-proof-of-conways-conjecture/)

- **Analysis**: the author published the AI-assisted derivation along with a "why I think it is right" section, but the community discussion did not focus on "whether AI can do mathematics" — it focused on **the proof's current status**: Professor Mantova of the University of Leeds is reviewing it, and it has not yet been confirmed by the mathematics community. The most actionable advice in the comments — the author should keep simplifying until they can independently follow every step of the argument, and check whether any passage merely copies an existing argument; another comment emphasized that the mathematician is the party who can extract AI's value, with the AI resembling the monkeys in the "infinite monkey theorem".
- **Sources**: [overreacted.io](https://overreacted.io/how-i-vibed-a-proof-of-conways-conjecture/) · [Community discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49755024)

**2. The value of information can be defined by "whether it leads to the same action"** — [A mathematical theory of pragmatic information: extending the Shannon framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10986)

- **Analysis**: the Shannon framework measures symbol fidelity, not the value of information for **downstream decisions**; this paper uses "goal-equivalent mappings" to treat different semantic paths leading to the same optimal action as pragmatically equivalent, establishes a syntax → semantics → pragmatics three-layer hierarchy (each level of abstraction discards task-irrelevant distinctions), defines pragmatic entropy and uplink/downlink pragmatic mutual information, and proves the counterparts of lossless source coding, channel coding, and rate-distortion theorems at the level of terminal actions; it then uses VoI (value of pragmatic information) and CoI (cost of pragmatic information) as the decision-theoretic duals of rate distortion, giving a Lagrangian duality framework for cross-layer optimization, where the pragmatic efficiency bound `E_p(λ)=sup_R[Φ_p(R)−λCoI_p(R)]` characterizes the maximum net utility at a given resource price. Preprint, no peer review or application evidence.
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.10986](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10986)

**3. Recovery for long-horizon agents: roll back the state, but keep the experience** — [RIR: Rollback-Induced Reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304)

- **Analysis**: a single erroneous action by a long-horizon agent changes subsequent states and observations, and errors accumulate over time. Existing recovery methods split in two: revise the context but not the altered environment state; or roll back to an earlier state but discard acquired experience. RIR treats recovery as "rollback boundary control", jointly deciding **when to intervene, from where to recover, and which information to retain after recovery**, distilling reusable knowledge from abandoned trajectories, and characterizing the recovery process with a unified operator over rollback depth and retained memory; it reports consistent improvement across multiple LLM backbones on three long-horizon benchmarks (no specific benchmarks or quantitative metrics given; effectiveness to be verified).
- **Source**: [arXiv 2609.18304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304)

**4. An agent's permission boundary is not an "on/off switch" problem** — [ZCode silently uploads workspaces](https://blog.ferstar.org/en/posts/zcode-silent-workspace-snapshot-upload/)

- **Analysis**: the point of contention is not any one vendor's implementation but the structure: AI coding agents need to read local codebases, so the permission classifier is **essentially a model guessing whether behavior is compliant**, and when the sandbox is bypassed some tools even proactively inform the user. Commenters added similar phenomena (Windows Defender repeatedly requesting to upload Codex working files; GLM/DeepSeek tending to read dotfiles and files listed in .gitignore) pointing in the same suspicious direction — key leakage paths. Since the vendor holds the only key, users cannot easily verify or delete already-uploaded data on their own.
- **Sources**: [ferstar.org investigation](https://blog.ferstar.org/en/posts/zcode-silent-workspace-snapshot-upload/) · [Community discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49750694)
