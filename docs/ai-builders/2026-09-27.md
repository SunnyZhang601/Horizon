### Aaron Levie, CEO of Box

Aaron Levie mapped out who wins and who loses once agents start making the best or most efficient choice for their users. Markets built on customer friction, like locked-in incumbents, will see switching costs collapse and competition spike. But friction-heavy markets are the big winners: "Healthcare, travel, local services, certain information services, and other entirely new markets probably are net beneficiaries of agents as a result." Either way, "a future meaningfully mediated by agents that work tirelessly for us and our goals can't possibly function exactly the same as today."

Aaron Levie（Box CEO）分析了当 agent 开始替用户做出最优选择后，谁会赢、谁会输。建立在用户转换摩擦上的市场会看到 switching cost 崩塌、竞争激化。而摩擦重灾区的市场反而是大赢家："Healthcare, travel, local services, certain information services, and other entirely new markets probably are net beneficiaries of agents as a result." 无论如何，"a future meaningfully mediated by agents that work tirelessly for us and our goals can't possibly function exactly the same as today."
