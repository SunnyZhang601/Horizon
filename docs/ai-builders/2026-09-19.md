**Aaron Levie, CEO of Box**

In a wide post on personal agents, he argues they are the ultimate manifestation of "build something that agents want". A product like Muse works when you can hand off a task and trust the agent to complete it end to end, which means the agent must operate with your tools, use its own, navigate your site, and transact. His key point: the new attention products compete for is not the user but the agent, so the tools that let agents order food, handle ecommerce, book flights and work with your data will get used the most. He calls this the biggest shakeup in consumer tech since the App Store.

在一篇关于 personal agent 的长帖中，他认为 personal agent 是 "build something that agents want" 的终极体现。像 Muse 这样的产品，只有当你能把任务交出去并相信 agent 端到端完成时才算成立，这意味着 agent 必须能用你的工具或自己的工具、浏览你的网站、完成交易。他的核心观点：产品要争取的新注意力不再是用户本身，而是 agent，因此那些最能支持 agent 订餐、处理电商、订机票和管理数据的工具会被使用得最多。他称之为自 App Store 以来消费级科技最大的一次震荡。

**Nikunj Kothari, partner at FPV Ventures**

He spends about two hours a day programming, building side projects and trying new repos, and gets a DM almost daily asking why a VC bothers. His answer: things move so fast that you cannot learn what is coming without hands-on time. His three-layer framework for investing in the AI era: understand model capabilities to see where the next models improve (and there is still alpha in reading model research papers), see what infrastructure models need for longer horizon tasks, and understand harness capabilities that let models build autonomous companies. His push to everyone: start tinkering daily, and after ten days you will be equally obsessed.

他每天花大约两小时编程、做 side project、试用新仓库，几乎每天都会收到私信问一个 VC 为什么要这样做。他的回答：现在节奏太快，不上手就根本学不到接下来会发生什么。他给 AI 时代投资的框架分三层：理解 model capabilities 才能看出下一代模型会在哪里提升（读 model research 论文仍有 alpha），看清模型完成更长 horizon 任务需要什么 infra，理解让模型能构建自主公司的 harness capabilities。他对所有人的建议：每天动手试一试，坚持十天你也会同样着迷。
