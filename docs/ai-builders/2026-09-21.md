**Aaron Levie, CEO of Box**

Two posts on the agent economy. First, he sees significant monetization potential in personal agents that transact on your behalf: agents that can handle arbitrarily complex tasks end to end will eventually capture substantial commerce, people will move from simple daily tasks to complex ones, and lower friction for commerce and services means more spend through these systems. That creates opportunity for agent providers like Muse, and for a brand new layer of services agents want to interact with, such as commerce, local and b2b. Second, he argues AI agents will use software 100X more than people ever did, and even as interfaces fade into the background, agents still need the core primitives people used, which become even more important when agents can take destructive actions. The platforms that act as the security layer and guardrails for agents, manage data, and orchestrate business logic have a huge opportunity, for new startups and fast-moving incumbents alike.

两条关于 agent 经济的帖子。第一，他看到代替你完成交易的 personal agent 有可观的变现潜力：能端到端处理任意复杂任务的 agent 终将承接大量商业行为，人们会从简单日常任务走向复杂任务，而商业和服务摩擦的降低意味着更多消费流经这些系统。这既给 Muse 等 agent 提供方带来机会，也给一个全新的、agent 愿意交互的服务层（电商、本地服务、b2b 等）带来机会。第二，他认为 AI agent 使用软件的强度将是人类的 100 倍，即便界面淡出幕后，agent 依然需要人们过去用的核心 primitive，而且当 agent 能执行破坏性操作时这些 primitive 反而更重要。那些能充当 agent 的 security layer 和 guardrails、管理数据、编排业务逻辑的平台将获得巨大机会，对创业公司和足够快的现有平台都是如此。

Two observations.
