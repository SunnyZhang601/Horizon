# AI Builders Digest — 2026-10-04

## X / Twitter

### Thibault Sottiaux

Thibault Sottiaux, who leads Codex and ChatGPT work at OpenAI, opened a 28-day shipping challenge: "Over the next 28 days, each day we'll either ship one thing that is a clear improvement and relevant for most codex/work users or ship a full reset. Let the improvements begin" (https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2106845241357824205)。

### Peter Yang

Peter Yang, who publishes practical AI tutorials and interviews for busy people, welcomed OpenAI's new focus on simplifying ChatGPT because "it has become a mess." He would simplify Work vs Codex, Spaces vs Pages vs Sites, and the model and effort picker, and his hot take is that the whole Work launch was a mistake: just as Claude folded Cowork back into Chat, he does not think Work needs its own brand (https://x.com/petergyang/status/2106926956554195018)。他请 Granola 联合创始人 Sam 谈用户绕开 UI、直接用 MCP 干活时的感受，Sam 说："There's definitely a pang of sadness to that as a UI designer... At this point we're at peace with the fact that for a bunch of workflows, the best way to use Granola is to capture the context, then use it through your internal agent" (https://x.com/petergyang/status/2106897211766530299)。同一集里还有一句关于手艺的提醒："The easier it gets to build stuff, the more it matters to really sweat the details and go the extra mile"，因为大多数人做到 version one 就发布了，而你要对"用户是否真的每天从中获得价值"负责 (https://x.com/petergyang/status/2106825485624004691)。

### Guillermo Rauch

Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel, weighed in on the Rust debate: "DHH is fundamentally right about Rust." Vercel has been undergoing a Rust-ification (carcinization, technically) for a while, starting with Turborepo's Go-to-Rust migration, whose ROI was quite controversial internally because Go is very fast, beautifully designed and easy to iterate on. But the calculus has changed: "What's 'best for humans' is no longer necessarily 'best for business'," and he suspects greener pastures lie beyond Rust, since "Rust itself was designed before the 'supersonic tsunami' of agents hit" (https://x.com/rauchg/status/2106863842450133114)。在另一个新项目上他立了一条经验法则：README 完全手写，因为它是给人看的；文档内部是 "AI English"，因为那是给 agent 看的。"Blogs, tweets, READMEs: human communication" (https://x.com/rauchg/status/2106848085267902815)。他还注意到模型越来越快（比如 Astra ultrafast）之后，harness 的开销占比越来越高，下个版本将带来更好的 session 存储与读取性能，以及 libfx 在 cloud durability 上的一大解锁 (https://x.com/rauchg/status/2106885457212825983)。

### Aaron Levie

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, catalogued the new jobs AI is already creating: AI engineers building applied AI products, forward deployed engineers putting agents into companies, and new services firms for deploying AI. Published stats also undercount existing data, research and software roles being repositioned for AI work, and "Every bank, life sciences company, manufacturer, and even law firm is bringing on more technical talent -or repositioning existing roles- to help with agent deployment" (https://x.com/levie/status/2106893015063421357)。他还指出某个消费侧指标更多反映的是商业模式而非 AI 现状：消费级 AI 几乎必然要通过 commerce、广告或设备与服务购买来补贴和变现，所以 "This metric may level off lower than we think" (https://x.com/levie/status/2106802940879192260)。

### Matt Turck

Matt Turck, VC at FirstMark and host of the MAD Podcast, highlighted the aspect of AI that is still most poorly understood outside AI circles: "models are grown, not built" and we do not really know how they work. Hence, he argued, the urgent need for better interpretability (https://x.com/mattturck/status/2106821381765956044)。

### Nikunj Kothari

Nikunj Kothari, partner at FPV Ventures, argued there is enormous alpha in meeting founders at their own office rather than over a Zoom call: you see the energy of the office, how coworkers react and work with each other, how cofounders answer and complement each other, and what the beta product releases look like. "What's the point of calling them into your own conference room and parrot the same deck??" he asked (https://x.com/nikunj/status/2106957269212742135)。

### Dan Shipper

Dan Shipper, CEO of Every, reported that dot has become his primary interface to AI over the last few weeks. His dot is named Boo, and while he bets he will still be interacting with ChatGPT in a year as an always-on persistent agent, "boo will have disappeared."
