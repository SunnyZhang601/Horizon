## PODCASTS

The Takeaway: Stefano Ermon believes diffusion models will beat autoregressive models at inference because of a hardware-level argument, not just a research preference: the more parallel solution is the one that wins.

On No Priors, the Stanford professor and co-founder and CEO of Inception traces his path from 2019, when he and his PhD student Yang Song developed score-based generative models that became the foundation of modern diffusion. Their 2024 breakthrough proved a diffusion language model could match an autoregressive model's quality at GPT-2 scale while generating text about 10x faster. His argument for why diffusion wins at inference: autoregressive generation is sequential, one token at a time, which maps poorly to GPUs and is memory-bound, while diffusion processes many tokens in parallel, the same kind of workload that training already exploits efficiently. In his words, "the bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

Inception, around 50 people and two years old, serves its Mercury models in production today, claiming parity with the speed-optimized flash-class models from frontier labs while being significantly faster. Because no existing engine can serve diffusion LLMs, the company built its own serving engine, which has become core IP. A named customer case: the voice agent company Open Call previously served its LLM on Cerebras custom chips to hit its latency budget, then switched to Mercury and got equivalent speed on standard NVIDIA GPUs, meaning more availability and lower cost. Ermon adds that diffusion models are structurally easier to control, since coarse-to-fine generation lets you steer outputs mid-generation against a reward function, something autoregressive models cannot do until generation completes.

On academia, he points out that FlashAttention and DPO both came out of his lab as rotation projects, arguing that academia remains where contrarian bets get made. His honest caveat: an immature ecosystem means everything from kernels to post-training tooling had to be built in house, which is both moat and burden.

在 No Priors 节目中,这位斯坦福教授、Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO 回顾了自己的路径:2019 年,他和博士生 Yang Song 提出的 score-based generative models 成为了现代 diffusion 的基石。2024 年的突破证明了 diffusion 语言模型可以在 GPT-2 规模上匹配 autoregressive 模型的质量,同时生成速度快约 10 倍。他认为 diffusion 在 inference 上获胜的理由是硬件层面的:autoregressive 逐 token 顺序生成,映射到 GPU 上效率很差且受限于 memory bandwidth,而 diffusion 并行处理大量 token,与 training 阶段已经被高效利用的工作负载同构。用他的话说,"the bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

Inception 成立两年、约 50 人,Mercury 模型已在生产环境服务,声称质量与前沿实验室的 flash 级速度优化模型持平,同时显著更快。因为没有现成引擎能服务 diffusion LLM,公司自建了 serving engine,这如今成了核心 IP。一个具名客户案例:voice agent 公司 Open Call 过去为了满足延迟预算,把 LLM 部署在 Cerebras 定制芯片上,后来切换到 Mercury,在标准 NVIDIA GPU 上获得了同等速度,意味着更高的可用性和更低的成本。Ermon 补充说,diffusion 模型在结构上更易控制:粗到细的生成方式允许在生成中途根据 reward function 进行 steering,而 autoregressive 模型必须等生成结束才能评判。

谈到学术界,他指出 FlashAttention 和 DPO 都出自他的实验室,而且最初只是 rotation project;他坚持学术界仍是逆共识下注的地方。他坦率的保留意见是:生态不成熟意味着从 kernel 到 post-training 工具链都得自研,这既是护城河,也是负担。
