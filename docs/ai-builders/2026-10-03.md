### Aaron Levie

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, mapped how bimodal agent adoption still is: coding and coding-adjacent tasks have taken off while everything else lags. Even within coding, only a small percentage of developers deploy background agents working on projects in parallel; the rest still work with agents one to one. Real agentic adoption in knowledge work requires workflows rebuilt, data wired up in new ways, new practices for accountability and liability, governance and compliance changes, and security upgrades. "We're still so unbelievably early... This is why you can basically expect 100X more agent adoption from what we've seen so far" (https://x.com/levie/status/2106583814709633413)。

### Ryo Lu

Ryo Lu, a designer who has worked on Cursor, Notion and Stripe, published a long essay on "convergence to the mean": when everyone looks at everyone else to decide what to make, we eventually stop making anything of our own. AI makes this loop frictionless, but "access to every perspective isn't the same as having one." His answer is to approach making things more like artists, and he closes with the question: "what is the point of everyone being able to make something, if we all end up making the same thing?" (https://x.com/ryolu_/status/2106337039201505453)
