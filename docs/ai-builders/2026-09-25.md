### Boris Cherny, creator of Claude Code at Anthropic

Boris Cherny says Tag, the Claude-powered Slack bot, now writes more than half of his pull requests every day and does roughly all of his data analysis, and he shared the exact prompts he uses to give it that much autonomy. He tells it to react to threads with a checkmark when resolved, to reproduce every reported bug end to end and open a pull request tagging the right team, and to brainstorm around a hundred hypotheses about strange data and "spend 10m tokens or so digging very deep" before drawing a chart. ### Aaron Levie, CEO of Box

Aaron Levie argued that evals are the overlooked bottleneck for enterprise AI adoption. His thesis in one line: "You can't automate what you can't measure. Aaron Levie（Box CEO）认为 eval 是企业采用 AI 时被忽视的瓶颈。一句话概括他的论点："You can't automate what you can't measure. This means that evals are one of the gates to diffusion of AI in the enterprise." 他指出，大多数企业今天连自己非确定性流程的运转情况都说不清楚，更不用说 agent 在替他们做的工作。所有的变更、升级和部署都以好的 eval 为前提，所以 eval 至关重要，随着领域专用 eval 的增多，这也是个巨大的机会。

### Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw and OpenAI contributor

Peter Steinberger shared a lesson from scaling OpenClaw: the biggest design mistake was using synchronous SQLite access, which was fine when one agent reported to you on Slack or iMessage but limiting now that one agent runs 50 sessions in parallel and a whole team works on it. His fix is ambitious: a goal running with Astra that has so far landed 575 pull requests moving everything to async workers, shipped as progress is made. His verdict: "Pretty insane how even huge refactors are no longer scary."

Peter Steinberger（OpenClaw 与 OpenAI 贡献者）分享了扩展 OpenClaw 的教训：最大的设计失误是用了同步 SQLite 访问，当只有一个 agent 通过 Slack 或 iMessage 向你汇报时没问题，但现在一个 agent 要并行跑 50 个 session、整个团队都在上面工作时就成了瓶颈。他的解法相当大胆：用 Astra 跑一个 goal，至今已经合并了 575 个把一切迁移到 async workers 的 PR，边做边发布。他的结论是："Pretty insane how even huge refactors are no longer scary."
