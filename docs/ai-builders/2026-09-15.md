## PODCASTS

The Takeaway: Aaron Levie argues that the trillion-dollar question in AI is not model intelligence but the bridge between models and enterprise workflows, and that the companies who own that bridge, not the labs, will capture most of the applied value.

On Training Data, Box founder and CEO Aaron Levie lays out why application companies have become the new labs. Two years ago being an "LLM wrapper" sounded like a death sentence; today he sees a vast gap between what models can do and what enterprises need, spanning data connections, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, change management, and legacy systems. His analogy: AWS and GCP created trillions in infrastructure value, but Snowflake and Databricks built trillions on top. He predicts the same for intelligence, and estimates a trillion dollars of applied-layer value is up for grabs.

A recurring theme is incentives. When you hand a task to an agent, you want it cost-optimized with accuracy held constant, which favors a vendor that does not care which model runs it. He expects token subsidization by labs to be temporary, since public-market economics will eventually apply. On open weights he offers a three-part answer: adoption is higher than people think, lower than enterprises actually want, and much lower than it will be in five years. He foresees a duality where frontier models orchestrate while cheap open models farm out long-tail work, so closed and open labs both grow because the pie is exploding.

The most concrete numbers come from Box itself: a harness tuned to its file system, permission structures, and search that beats raw model APIs on accuracy and latency, with the Complex Work eval as its benchmark. His boldest claim: "in five years from now, I would bet, like, 90% of all tokens in the enterprise are things that a user never kicked off." And his advice to students is blunt: get wired in, because "You're either, like, a year ahead or a year behind simply based on your feed."

Box 创始人兼 CEO Aaron Levie 在 Training Data 上解释了为什么应用公司正在成为新的"实验室"。两年前,"LLM wrapper"听起来像死刑判决;如今他看到模型能力与企业真实需求之间存在巨大鸿沟,涵盖数据连接、human-in-the-loop 检查点、change management 和遗留系统。他的类比是:AWS 和 GCP 创造了数万亿的基础设施价值,但 Snowflake 和 Databricks 又在其上再造了数万亿;智能领域也会如此,他估算 applied layer 有上万亿美元的价值等着被拿走。

贯穿全篇的主题是激励结构。把任务交给 agent 时,你希望它在保证准确率的前提下做成本优化,这天然利好"不关心任务跑在哪个模型上"的厂商。他预计实验室对 token 的补贴只是暂时的,因为上市公司终究要服从资本市场的经济学。对 open weights,他给出三段式回答:采用率比人们以为的高,比企业真正想要的低,又远低于五年后的水平。他预计会出现一种双重结构:前沿模型做 orchestration,廉价的 open model 承接长尾任务,于是闭源和开源同时增长,因为整张饼在爆炸式变大。

最具体的数字来自 Box 自己:一个针对其文件系统、权限结构和搜索调优过的 harness,在准确率和延迟上都优于直接调用模型 API,基准是 Complex Work eval。他最大胆的判断是:"in five years from now, I would bet, like, 90% of all tokens in the enterprise are things that a user never kicked off。"对学生的建议也非常直接:要 wired in,因为"You're either, like, a year ahead or a year behind simply based on your feed."
