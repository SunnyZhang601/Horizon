### Robotics

**① Self-calibration of joint zero positions / encoder offsets is becoming a standard capability** —— [Lower-limb joint offset self-calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02306) · [OmniCalib](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19582)

- **Analysis**: Two papers landing on the same day, with complementary approaches. The former uses only **onboard encoders + a pelvis IMU**, and under static double support writes "the foot-to-foot transform from forward kinematics should be constant while both feet are planted" as a constraint, solving the 12-dimensional zero-offsets by minimizing how that quantity disperses across poses; the key contribution is the **observability analysis** — parallel pitch axes introduce rotational coupling, so pose residuals can only observe the sum of pitch offsets, and knee priors from standing poses are needed to anchor weakly observable pitch chains, hence "whether the pose excitation is sufficient" directly determines which offsets are solvable. The latter (OmniCalib) turns the same class of problem into a whole-machine workflow: task and parameter block matching → check observability first → write only **supported corrections** back into the CAD, covering the 14 arm joint zero positions and all RGB-D extrinsics. (Source nature: both **Author's claim**, preprints)

**② Scaling law for bipedal robots: body mass scales as L², not L³** —— [Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22560)

- **Analysis**: The paper surveys existing legged robots and finds mass actually scales with the square of leg length (the isometric assumption predicts L³); using two bipedal robots that share a quasi-passive morphology and differ sixfold in leg length in a controlled comparison under a unified 3D simulation, it obtains velocity ∝ L^½ and torque τ ∝ mL to sustain walking (hence roughly ∝L³ when m∝L², and roughly ∝L⁴ when m∝L³). The authors also note that the foot size that permits walking scales roughly linearly with leg length, but **the velocity-maximizing foot shape and each robot's gait mechanics cannot be transferred by scaling**. (Source nature: **Author's claim**)

**③ Touch: two routes — "fewer transducers in hardware" and "fewer tokens in representation" — advancing simultaneously** —— [Metal ultrasonic waveguide touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02827) · [STAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12549) · [DexTouch-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20649) · [ZeroTouch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21726)

- **Analysis**: On the hardware side, the metal ultrasonic waveguide approach interrogates an entire piece of metal with **a single proximal transducer**, using the reflection/transmission coefficient ratio for force estimation and the distribution of reflected energy for material discrimination; extended to 2D metal sheets it can also localize contact — effectively converting the cost of "large-area sensing" from "many transducers + much wiring" into "one excitation + signal interpretation". On the learning side, STAR uses **sparse global tactile tokens + sparse future-tactile prediction** to attack the sparsity of touch along the spatial/temporal/information dimensions; DexTouch-WM instead shares a sensing layout between human and machine and treats human interaction as a transferable supervision signal for contact dynamics. (Source nature: both **Author's claim**)

- **Contact-rich simulation and diagnostics**: CRISP (a high-fidelity contact-rich robot simulation platform), [CoMET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21803) (GPU-parallel mode evaluation to accelerate contact-rich motion planning), [ProTracer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21369) (training-free proprioceptive manipulation fault diagnosis) — all **Author's claim**, together pointing to "contact modeling remains the main sim-to-real gap".

### Embedded

**① EventCV: event cameras finally get "a single toolchain"** —— [Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21330)

- **Analysis**: The pain point of event cameras is integration, not algorithms: no plug-and-play drivers, mutually incompatible event-stream file formats, and most projects depending on research-grade bespoke code. EventCV uses a **Rust core + OpenCV-style Python bindings** to put driver capture (integrating Neuromorphic Drivers), denoising and geometric transforms, corner detection and unsupervised feature learning, contrast-maximization motion estimation, video-to-event simulation, and ONNX inference into one toolchain, and ran three robotics cases on a **Jetson Orin AGX**. (Source nature: **Author's claim**; the performance multipliers are the authors' own statement, **To be verified**)

**② How SLAM fails in low light has now been quantified** —— [Noctif3R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21114)

- **Analysis**: The most valuable part of the paper is the **failure measurement**: on all nine darkest levels, DROID-SLAM returned full-length trajectories "containing no information about camera motion" on all nine levels, VGGT-SLAM / CUT3R on eight, pi³ on seven, and DPV-SLAM on four — i.e. the systems appear to be running while their output is uninformative. The authors' own SYS had the lowest error when it could track but the narrowest coverage; on a real video where 86.5% of frames were fully black, every configuration stopped after the bright opening. (Source nature: **Author's claim**)

**③ Offline voice commands on the edge** —— [Jarvis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21109): offline command recognition for autonomous racing, with no cloud dependency (source nature: **Author's claim**). Related to the "on-device inference + low latency" line of work.

**② Safety filtering need not be a performance cost** —— [Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18082)

- **Analysis**: Common practice is to wrap a safety filter around the RL policy and assume this sacrifices performance. Using SC-MDP (which requires avoiding catastrophic failures **categorically** rather than with high probability) and the filtered MDP, the paper proves that learning in a filtered MDP is categorically safe, standard convergence guarantees carry over, and the optimal policy executed through the same filter achieves the same asymptotic return as the optimal safe policy of the SC-MDP — the takeaway is to **train and deploy with the most permissive available filter**. (Source nature: **Author's claim**, theoretical result + validation on Safety Gymnasium)
